> **v19:** use this file for its **method** — how weak, literal ideas were replaced by stronger ones that explain the
> line — not for its symbols. Video 05 used a heart as a spine symbol for warmth; since 5 Oct 2026 heart imagery and
> other simple symbols are out (Thomas: "the smartphone with the large heart feels too basic"). In v19 the warmth half of
> this spine would be shown through behaviour — Mom sitting down beside her daughter, kneeling to her level, a hand
> reaching out (`acting-and-interaction-v19.md`) — and the gate would stay as a strong recurring object whose meaning
> changes each time it returns (`props-symbols-metaphors-v19.md`). Its rooms and colours were the v17 look; v19 places are
> outline cues on the light ground (`style-and-colour-v19.md`).

**Spine (as delivered):** the heart (warmth) and the gate (structure). Mom starts with only the heart, learns to hold the gate, ends holding both; the last frame mirrors the first (same living room, the tablet untouched on its shelf).
**Motifs (as delivered):** the tablet (shelf → handed over → still no → screen dark → untouched), the gate (floats away on balloons → clicks shut → rattled → shut below at night → leaned on), the heart, the storm (purple), the clock, the teddy (Mom's childhood). The tablet and the gate are the model for v19 motifs: each return is a new state with a new meaning.

**Render history:** first full re-stage looked "the same as before" (3/10) → cold open rebuilt with fixed rooms and growing storm (6.5/10) → angles described as what the camera sees, bigger characters (≈8/10) → creative pass on ideas → accepted.
